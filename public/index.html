A note on expertise

I’m not here to be an expert at everything.

This is a place for staying curious, beginning new things and sharing honest progress—especially the awkward, useful parts before something looks effortless.

Software taught me how to break big problems into smaller ones. Training taught me that consistency beats intensity. Making things reminds me that the fastest route to clarity is often a rough first attempt.

Hey Subrat is where those lessons meet. No perfect playbook. Just experiments, repetitions and notes from the road.

Stay curious. Keep moving. Make the thing.
